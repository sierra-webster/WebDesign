OUR STORY & MISSION

Who We Are

The Bookshelf is a new site for readers and book recommendations. Our mission is to help readers discover books they love and get more out of reading. Serving young adults who don’t see themselves as readers, including people who haven’t picked up a book in a while, or who have never been interested in reading.

A Few Things You Can Do On The Bookshelf:

  • ✦ See what books your friends are reading.
  • ✦ Track the books you might have seen in theaters.
  • ✦ Check out the latest books that are hitting the shelves.
  • ✦ Keep our book categories up to date without a complicated cataloging system.
  • ✦ Find out if a book is a good fit for you.

Hours & Locations

Digital Hub: Online-first platform open 24/7 for book discovery and reading lists.
Community Events: Pop-up book swaps and casual reading meetups hosted across America. Follow our social feeds for upcoming dates and cities!
