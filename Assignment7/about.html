OUR STORY & MISSION

Who We Are

The Bookshelf is a new site for readers and book recommendations. Our mission is to help readers discover books they love and get more out of reading.

A Few Things You Can Do On The Bookshelf:

  • ✦ See what books your friends are reading.
  • ✦ Track the books you might have seen in theaters.
  • ✦ Check out the latest books that are hitting the shelves.
  • ✦ Keep our book categories up to date without a complicated cataloging system.
  • ✦ Find out if a book is a good fit for you.
