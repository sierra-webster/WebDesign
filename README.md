# The Bookshelf — Project 2 README

## Client 2 Project Notes

* **Who They Are:** A literacy and reading organization
* **Who They Want to Reach:** Young adults, particularly those who don't currently identify as readers
* **What They Hope to Achieve:** Encourage more young adults to discover and enjoy reading books.
* **What the Website Should Offer:** Engaging book discovery, accessible recommendations, and opportunities to explore reading based on personal interests.
* **What to Keep in Mind:** Challenge assumptions about who reads, avoid intimidating newcomers, and make reading feel relevant, enjoyable, and approachable.
